The Crate

Download your SoundCloud, Apple Music or Spotify playlist straight to your USB.

How it works

What it does

Paste a playlist link. Every day at your Auto sync time (or when you hit Sync Now) it grabs any new tracks into Music › SoundCloud Sync on your Mac, then copies them to your USB whenever it's plugged in. It only adds tracks; nothing on your USB gets deleted.

Where it looks, in order

  1. SoundCloud: the artist's original file, if they turned downloads on.
  2. Free download links in the track description (Dropbox, Google Drive and so on).
  3. Your Downloads folder: anything you bought or unlocked yourself gets picked up and filed.
  4. Soulseek, the file-sharing network a lot of DJs use. Download only; it never shares your files.

Anything it can't find stays under Still to get, with links to Bandcamp, Beatport, Traxsource, Juno and Qobuz.

Spotify and Apple Music only stream, so for those it reads the track list and gets the files from steps 3 and 4.

Quality

Lossless (WAV, FLAC, AIFF) or 320 kbps MP3. Nothing lower is kept. It never records a stream.

SoundCloud originals come as the artist uploaded them; anything under 320 is marked in your list. Soulseek files have to match the track's length within 5 seconds, so you get the same mix, not an extended or radio edit.

Is it safe?

Every file is checked before it's kept: it has to actually be audio, and it's virus scanned. Anything else goes to a Quarantine folder and never reaches your USB.

Why the first sync is slow

Soulseek only allows so many searches a minute, so a big playlist takes a few hours the first time. After that it only looks for new tracks. Tracks nobody has are tried again after 1, 2, 4, then 7 days.

Do I need to keep it open?

No, it runs in the background. Keep your Mac on and plugged in at your Auto sync time (if it's asleep, it syncs when it wakes up), and plug in your USB whenever you want the tracks.
