The Crate

Download your SoundCloud, Apple Music or Spotify playlist straight to your USB.

How it works

What it does

Gets every track in your playlist onto your Mac, then your USB. Daily at your Auto sync time, or on Sync Now. It never deletes anything.

SoundCloud, Spotify or Apple Music

Paste any public playlist link. SoundCloud uses your browser's SoundCloud sign-in: only a signed-in account can download the artist's own file. Spotify and Apple Music need no sign-in: they only stream, so The Crate just reads the song list and finds each song elsewhere. Spotify shows the first 100 songs (copy the rest from the Spotify app and paste them in the same box), Apple Music the first 300.

Where it looks

  1. SoundCloud: the artist's original file
  2. Free download links in the description or behind the track's Free Download button
  3. The Downloads folder on your Mac: anything you bought
  4. Soulseek: download only, never shares your files

Not found? You get shop links. Spotify and Apple Music only stream, so those use steps 3 and 4.

Quality

Lossless or 320 kbps only, never a recorded stream. Soulseek files must match the track's length, so you get the same mix.

Your USB and decks

Songs go into one folder on the USB, SoundCloud Sync, and into a playlist folder called The Crate in Engine DJ, rekordbox and Serato. Your own files, folders and playlists are never moved, renamed or deleted. A USB that already has things on it is asked about first.

Ready for a gig

FLAC becomes AIFF (the same sound, in a file every club deck plays), missing covers and details are filled in, and your cues stay in step across decks. See Library Manager for each one and its Undo.

Is it safe?

Every file is checked to be real audio and virus scanned. Anything else is quarantined.

Why Full Disk Access?

For SoundCloud only. It uses the sign-in already in your browser, and macOS keeps that locked. Full Disk Access unlocks it. Your sign-in only ever goes to SoundCloud.

What it uses

Free, open-source tools on your Mac: yt-dlp (SoundCloud), sockseek (Soulseek), ClamAV (virus scan). No account, no AI, nothing uploaded.

Why does it take so long?

Soulseek limits searches, so a big playlist takes a few hours the first time. After that, only new tracks.

Do I keep it open?

No. Keep your Mac on at sync time and plug in your USB whenever.
