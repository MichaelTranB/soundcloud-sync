The Crate

Download your SoundCloud, Apple Music or Spotify playlist straight to your USB.

How it works

What it does

Gets every track in your playlist onto your Mac, then your USB. Daily at your Auto sync time, or on Sync Now. It never deletes anything.

Where it looks

  1. SoundCloud: the artist's original file
  2. Free download links in the description
  3. Your Downloads: anything you bought
  4. Soulseek: download only, never shares your files

Not found? You get shop links. Spotify and Apple Music only stream, so those use steps 3 and 4.

Quality

Lossless or 320 kbps only, never a recorded stream. Soulseek files must match the track's length, so you get the same mix.

Is it safe?

Every file is checked to be real audio and virus scanned. Anything else is quarantined.

Why Full Disk Access?

It uses the SoundCloud sign-in already in your browser, and macOS keeps that locked. Full Disk Access unlocks it. Your sign-in only ever goes to SoundCloud.

What it uses

Free, open-source tools on your Mac: yt-dlp (SoundCloud), sockseek (Soulseek), ClamAV (virus scan). No account, no AI, nothing uploaded.

Why does it take so long?

Soulseek limits searches, so a big playlist takes a few hours the first time. After that, only new tracks.

Do I keep it open?

No. Keep your Mac on at sync time and plug in your USB whenever.
