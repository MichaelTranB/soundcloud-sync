How it works

What it does

Gets every song The Crate downloaded ready to play: it works out each song's tempo, key and genres, then puts your songs and playlists into your DJ apps. You never drag anything in.

Where your songs show up

  1. Engine DJ, on your USB: Denon and Numark decks
  2. rekordbox, on this Mac: your Collection
  3. rekordbox, on your USB: under the USB in rekordbox, and on the newest club decks (CDJ-3000X, XDJ-AZ, OPUS-QUAD, OMNIS-DUO)
  4. Serato, on your USB: its crates

Everywhere, your playlists sit in one folder called The Crate. The songs themselves sit on the USB once, in The Crate's own folder.

USB folders

Inside The Crate's folder your songs sit in one folder, in EDM and Other, or by genre (EDM/Dubstep, Other/Hip Hop). You pick, and it asks before it moves anything.

  1. New songs are filed by themselves. A song moves when you change its genre.
  2. Playlists and cues follow. Nothing outside The Crate's folder is touched, and no song is copied twice.
  3. One folder puts every song back.
  4. Spotify Liked Songs keep their own folder and their own playlist. One joins a genre playlist when you give it a genre.

When it runs

By itself: after every sync, when you plug in your USB, and when you change a genre here. Or press Update decks now.

Genres

Each genre is a playlist on your decks. A song's genre comes from the file, from SoundCloud, or from its sound. Your own pick here always wins.

Song recognition

Listens to each song and looks it up by its sound. That fills in missing genres and warns you when a Soulseek file is really another song. Needs a free AcoustID key, once. Only a sound fingerprint leaves your Mac, never the song.

Club check

Tells you what a club's decks need from your USB before a gig. Older Pioneer decks (CDJ-3000, CDJ-2000NXS2) read an older list on the USB: The Crate keeps that one up too, once rekordbox has exported to the USB one time.

Copying from another USB

Plug a friend's USB in and a line shows up here. Open it, tick its playlists or folders, press Copy to my library.

  1. Songs land in your library first, so they get everything your own songs get: safety check, tempo, key, genres, every deck.
  2. A song you already have isn't copied twice. One you threw away stays away.
  3. Each playlist keeps its name. Hot cues set in rekordbox come along onto your USB's rekordbox list.
  4. The other USB is only read, never changed.

Listening and quality

Press ▶ on a song to hear it and see its waveform: tap the waveform to jump. Open in rekordbox hands the song to rekordbox for a look on the deck. Below gig quality means an MP3 under 320 kbps (or an AAC under 256): fine at home, thin on a club system. Find a better copy asks Soulseek, and you keep your file until the better one lands.

AIFF for club decks

Older club decks skip FLAC. So a new FLAC becomes an AIFF by itself: the same sound, bit for bit, with its cover, tags, playlists and cues. The FLAC goes to the Trash only once the AIFF is checked. Undo is on the song's row.

Cover and details

Fills only what is empty: cover, title, artist, album, genre, year. It looks at the song's own SoundCloud page, then Apple Music and MusicBrainz, and writes only when the sources agree. If they don't, you pick from two or three. Undo is on the song's row.

Cues

Hot cues, memory cues and loops you set anywhere reach everywhere: the Prime 4, rekordbox on this Mac, and your USB for club decks. So you set a cue once.

  1. Runs at every update. The newest edit wins, and nothing is ever deleted.
  2. Undo on the Cues line puts the last change back.
  3. Not carried: MP3 cues to or from the Prime 4, memory cues to the Prime 4, and beat grids. Each app keeps its own grid.

Backup and repair

Each time you plug in your USB, its deck libraries (cues, grids, playlists, history) and any song only it has are backed up to your iCloud Drive, or this Mac when iCloud is off. Lost or broken USB? Plug in a new one and press Repair this USB: every song, playlist and cue comes back. Erasing a USB is never done for you.

Your USB is asked first

A USB that already has things on it is not touched until you press Go ahead. After that, songs go only into The Crate's own folder and playlists into its own The Crate folder. Your files, folders and playlists are never moved, renamed or deleted. You can stop any time.

Is my library safe?

It only touches its own songs and its own The Crate folder. Your other playlists and grids are left alone. Each library is backed up before a change, and rekordbox's is never written while rekordbox is open: it catches up when you close it.

Make it yours

Options, at the top, holds every switch: what runs by itself, how the USB is laid out, your genres. Each one can be changed back.

What it uses

Free, open-source tools on your Mac: Essentia (tempo and key), Mutagen (song details), pyrekordbox (rekordbox's libraries), Chromaprint, AcoustID and MusicBrainz (song recognition). No AI, no account besides the AcoustID key.

USB foldersHow your songs sit inside The Crate's folder on your USB.

By genre files each song under its first genre, like EDM/Dubstep or Other/Hip Hop. A song with any EDM genre counts as EDM. New songs are filed by themselves.

All songs: with folders, turn this on for a list of every song, All songs.m3u8, beside them: each song is stored once, in its own folder, and your decks still list each song once. "Make copies instead" makes a folder with a second copy of every song (it needs the room; the USB keeps 5 GB free); "Keep one copy of each song" takes those copies off again after your Go ahead, each only once its twin is the same file byte for byte.

Any device: names a USB can't hold (with : or ?) get a look-alike sign, and the hidden "._" files a Mac leaves are taken off, so phones and other computers open the folder cleanly.

Where it sits: at the top of the USB, or inside a folder you already keep music in, so all your songs are in one part of the USB. Changing it moves only The Crate's folder.

Never: touches anything outside The Crate's own folder or deletes a song. Playlists and cues follow.

Undo: pick One folder and every song goes back.

GenresEach genre is a playlist on your decks. Tap one to see its songs.

A song's genre comes from the file, from SoundCloud, or from its sound. Your own pick always wins: press Edit on a song.

EDM (all) and Other (all) are playlists too: every EDM song in one, the rest in the other.

The amber ones are to-do lists: songs with no genre, no cover, still a FLAC, and so on.

Removing a genre only takes it off the list. No song is deleted, and a change reaches your decks in a few seconds.

SongsTick songs, then choose what to do with them.

Convert to AIFF: the same sound, in a file every club deck plays. The original goes to the Trash once the AIFF is checked; playlists and cues follow.

Add cover & details: fills only what is empty, and only when it's sure; otherwise you choose.

Put on USB: copies songs not on your USB yet into The Crate's own folder. Nothing else on it is moved or deleted.

Fix names: puts a mixed-up title and artist right, in the file and on your decks. It writes only when a music catalogue agrees; otherwise you choose.

Each one shows Undo on the song's row.
